# Reaction clips

No reaction clips ship with the engine. Drop short MP4 clips here and the engine cuts to them when a line fits:

| File | Used when the phrase has |
|---|---|
| confused.mp4 | a question, "wait", "why", "how" |
| shocked.mp4 | crash, disaster, danger, wrong, trouble |
| mindblown.mp4 | impossible, surprise, twist, punchline, flip |
| thinking.mp4 | brain, expect, knows, predict |

Each clip is used at most once per reel, three at most. Landscape clips of 2 to 4 seconds work best (640 x 400 is
plenty); sound is ignored. With no clips here, those lines get a text card or a replay instead.

Use clips you have the right to use: your own face reacting works well and makes the reels yours.
