# Music bed

No music ships with the engine. To put a quiet bed under the voice, save a track here as `bed.mp3`.

- It starts when the voice starts, loops if the reel is longer, and fades out at the end.
- Volume is 14 percent by default: `node_modules/.bin/tsx src/cli.ts settings set musicVolume 0.1`
- Use music you have the right to use. Platforms mute or limit videos that carry commercial recordings.

Without a file here the reel has the voice and the sound effects only, which also leaves room to add a
trending sound in the Instagram or TikTok app when you post.
